Who fixes your computer, how I work, and answers to common questions.谁来修你的电脑、怎么修，以及常见问题。

Hi, I'm Jie. I study electrical and computer engineering at the University of Michigan.你好，我是 Jie，在密歇根大学读电子与计算机工程。

I've been opening up and repairing my own laptops for years, including a full thermal rebuild of a high-end gaming laptop. I've also programmed an industrial robot arm on a factory production line. Most laptops don't need replacing. They need a careful cleaning or one new part, and that's what I do.我自己拆修笔记本很多年了，包括给高端游戏本重新做散热。我也在工厂产线上给工业机械臂写过程序。大多数电脑其实不用换新，只需要认真清理一次或换一个配件，这正是我做的事。

Questions常见问题

Do you need my password?需要我的开机密码吗？

No. I test with my own bootable USB drive, so your account stays private. Please back up important files before any repair.不需要。我用自己的启动 U 盘做测试，不会碰你的账户。维修前请备份重要资料。

What if something goes wrong?如果修坏了怎么办？

We check and photograph your laptop together before I start. If I damage something, I repair it or pay for it.开始前我们会一起检查并拍照记录。如果是我造成的损坏，我负责修好或赔偿。

Which computers do you work on?修哪些电脑？

Windows laptops and desktop PCs, from everyday laptops to gaming laptops like Alienware, Lenovo Legion, ASUS ROG, MSI and HP Omen. Harder-to-open models cost more, as shown on the Prices page. I don't take MacBooks yet.Windows 笔记本和台式机，从普通笔记本到外星人、拯救者、ROG、微星、暗影精灵等游戏本都可以。越难拆的机型价格越高，详见价格页。暂时不修 MacBook。

Which thermal compound should I pick?导热材料选哪种？

Standard paste is fine for most computers. For gaming laptops that run hot every day, a phase-change pad lasts much longer. If you're not sure, pick “Not sure” and I'll recommend one.大多数电脑用标准硅脂就够了。每天高负载的游戏本更适合相变片，耐用得多。不确定就选“不确定”，我来推荐。

Can I buy my own RAM or SSD?可以自己买内存或固态吗？

Yes. Ask me first and I'll recommend parts that fit your model and budget, for free. You can buy them yourself, or I'll buy them for you at cost after a deposit.可以。先问我，我会免费推荐适合你机型和预算的配件。你可以自己买，也可以付定金后由我按成本价代购。

How do I pay?怎么付款？

After the work is done, by Venmo, Zelle or cash. Jobs that need a part take a deposit before I order it.修好后用 Venmo、Zelle 或现金付款。需要买配件的，订货前先付定金。

Where do you serve?服务范围？

Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti and Novi. Pickup is free within 10 miles, or we can meet on campus.安娜堡、Ypsilanti 和 Novi。10 英里内免费上门，也可以在校园见面。
