Device type

How do I find my model?怎么查看电脑型号？
  1. Press the Windows key, type System Information (or msinfo32) and open it.按 Windows 键，搜索 系统信息（或 msinfo32）并打开。
  2. System Model is your computer's model. BaseBoard Product is the motherboard model.系统型号 就是电脑型号，主板产品 是主板型号。
  3. Or check the sticker on the bottom of the laptop, or the box it came in.也可以看笔记本底部的标签或包装盒。
  4. Desktops: check the label on the case. For a custom build, send me the motherboard model.台式机看机箱上的标签；组装机请发主板型号。
